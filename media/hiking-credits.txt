Hiking short — original stylized Blender snow edition / 2026

All characters, clothing, backpack, snowfields, mountain ridges, snow-covered
firs, rocks, signposts and animation are procedurally created by
scripts/render_hiking_stylized.py for this website.
No stock footage, photographic background, AI image or downloaded character is used.
Rendered with Blender 4.3 / Cycles, 864 x 576, 24 fps, 12 seconds.

Motion uses a two-segment inverse-kinematic leg solver, planted stance, lifted
swing phase, opposite arm swing, body sway and backpack follow-through.
The scenery and planted boots share one world-space speed. A perspective
tracking camera creates depth-dependent parallax: near snowbanks move faster
on screen than trees and distant mountain ridges. Both mountain layers move.
Repeating neighboring scenery tiles prevent popping at the camera edges.
Alternating boot impressions are revealed behind the hiker on the snow trail.

Ten walk cycles and repeating terrain tiles make the animation loop without
reversing the hiker. Run the renderer with --validate to check perspective
parallax and the 12-second scenery loop before rendering.

The editor displays four excerpts from the actual Blender script: snowfield,
leg IK, perspective/parallax, and Cycles frame rendering. Generate the excerpts
and source line numbers with scripts/build_hiking_code.py. Typing, chapter
captions and frame readout follow the pre-rendered video's playback time;
Blender is not executed in the browser. The full script is downloadable.
This is a stylized fictional character, not a likeness of Weijia Wu.
